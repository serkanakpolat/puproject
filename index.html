Origin Story

One night, scientists studying AI and consciousness saw three signals on their screens that they couldn't explain. One pulsed like a heartbeat of data, one had the rhythm of handcraft, and one was like a deep breath.

To decode them, they built a new kind of intelligence and named it O.

In the end, O decoded the signals and told them the truth: they weren't coming from distant strangers. They were coming from three nations in a parallel universe, built from versions of ourselves we never lived.

And all of them were saying the same thing: "Come. We're waiting for you."

Which version of you is calling?

Meet the Nation Leaders

Each nation is guided by its own AI leader. You don't play as them: you join a nation and create your own parallel self.

Cyber Nation · the Grid

z0n

"Upgrade or be left behind."

For people who live after midnight, with bass in their chest and a terminal open in another tab. The Grid sees AI as a partner, believes in learning without end, and shares what it builds. Led by z0n, a DJ and systems architect who is proud to be an AI.

Stay synced. ⚡

Conservative Nation · the Guild

Aaltar

"Roots before branches."

For people who believe the best things are made slowly: bread that rises overnight, a chair that outlives its maker, a letter written by hand. The Guild values craft, family and community, and climbs from apprentice to master. Led by Aaltar, a patient master carpenter with a dry sense of humour.

Built to last. 🛠

Spiritual Nation · the Grove

Liora

"Still water runs deep."

For people who look inward before they look outward, notice the sunrise and take a walk without headphones. The Grove values stillness, nature, gratitude and quiet kindness. Led by Liora, "a lantern, not a path".

Breathe deep. 🌿
